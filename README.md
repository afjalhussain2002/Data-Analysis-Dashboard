# Data-Analysis-Dashboard
An Excel-based Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard that visualizes sales data across different months, cities, product categories, and salespersons. This project uses Excel charts, Pivot Tables, and slicers to identify sales trends, compare performance, and generate business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify business trends, evaluate sales performance, and generate actionable business recommendations.

An interactive dashboard was developed using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to help understand sales performance across different dimensions.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse monthly sales performance.
- Identify top-performing and low-performing cities.
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Evaluate product-wise revenue.
- Analyse individual salesperson performance.
- Identify business improvement opportunities.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Slicers
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

1. Monthly Sales Analysis – Tracks sales trends across different months.
2. City-wise Sales Analysis – Compares sales contributions across cities.
3. Category-wise Sales Analysis – Evaluates revenue across product categories.
4. Product-wise Sales Analysis – Identifies products generating higher revenue.
5. Salesperson Performance – Compares individual salesperson contributions.
6. Interactive Filtering – Uses slicers to filter and explore sales records.

💡 Key Business Insights

- Electronics contributes the largest share of category revenue.
- Furniture and Accessories have opportunities for sales improvement.
- Mumbai contributes a higher share of sales than other cities.
- March shows relatively lower sales compared with previous months.
- Manoj has the lowest salesperson sales contribution.

🚀 Business Recommendations

- Introduce targeted promotional campaigns during low-sales periods.
- Improve furniture sales through affordable pricing, EMI options, and product bundles.
- Increase marketing efforts in lower-performing cities.
- Encourage cross-selling of complementary products.
- Provide targeted training and mentoring to improve salesperson performance.
